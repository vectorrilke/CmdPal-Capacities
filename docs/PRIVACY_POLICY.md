# Privacy Policy

**Capacities Command Palette Extension**

## Summary

The Capacities Command Palette Extension does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

## How It Works

- You provide your Capacities API token locally (stored securely by Windows)
- All operations communicate directly with your Capacities account
- No data is sent to any external service except Capacities
- The extension does not use analytics, telemetry, or tracking

## What We Don't Do

❌ No personal data collection  
❌ No telemetry or analytics  
❌ No advertising or tracking  
❌ No data sharing with third parties  
❌ No user profiling  

## Your Data

All data you work with remains within your Capacities account. The extension simply provides a convenient Command Palette interface to your existing Capacities workspace.
